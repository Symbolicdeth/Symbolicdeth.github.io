Purple Rain

A song by Prince and The Revolution

Introduction

Purple Rain is a song by American musician Prince and his backing band The Revolution. It is the title track from the 1984 album of the same name, which also serves as the soundtrack album for the 1984 film.

Origins

Originally written as a country song and intended as a collaboration with Stevie Nicks, Prince explained the song's imagery in terms of red and blue blending into purple. He connected the purple rain with the end of the world, being with someone you love, and letting faith guide you.

Recording

The song was recorded during a benefit concert for the Minnesota Dance Theatre at the First Avenue nightclub in Minneapolis on August 3, 1983. The performance became the source of the version released on the album.

Reception and Legacy

The song has appeared on several major lists of acclaimed music. Rolling Stone ranked it among its 500 Greatest Songs of All Time, while Q magazine and Pitchfork also recognized its influence.
